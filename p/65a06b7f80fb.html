General productivity — deployment

Supercemento · September 2026

Objective. Deploy Claude, train the users, and work alongside them to apply it to their daily tasks.

Wave standard

Dates are for Wave 2.

Step What
Sector presentationThe plan is presented to the area. Participants are defined
Users setupHardware check that the standard machine runs Claude Desktop. Seats are bought, and each selected user gets a seat and what they need to install the application
Group presentationThe group sees the tool and gets the training plan. Two blocks, 12.5 hours on Anthropic Academy
Self-paced trainingTwo weeks. The foundations first, then the application. Users work with the tool while they train
Follow-up meetingsTwo, a week apart, while the training runs
DiscoveryTwo weeks, from the second follow-up. Each user collects the recurring tasks from their own work and works them through, with examples from their own area
ImplementationFrom the second follow-up onward. One to one: ways of working, automations, and applying the tool to each user's own tasks

Plan

Groups of up to six users.

Training

Anthropic Academy.

WeekCoursesContentTime
1 AI Capabilities and Limitations Build an accurate mental model of what large language models can and cannot do: next-token prediction, knowledge, working memory, steerability, and context limits 3.5 hr
AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations Learn to collaborate with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely using the 4D framework: Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence 4 hr
2 Claude 101 Learn to use Claude for everyday work, from your first conversation and effective prompting to projects, artifacts, skills, and connected tools 2.5 hr
Introduction to Claude Cowork Delegate multi-step work to Claude in Cowork: set up workspaces, give context, run task loops, and use plugins for research, documents, and browser tasks 2.5 hr
