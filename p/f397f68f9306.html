AI training — IT

Supercemento · August 2026

Summary

This document contains the proposed training for the IT department, as part of the AI integration plan for Supercemento.

Three blocks on Anthropic Academy, 18.75 hours. From how a language model works, through the Claude app, to building agents that reach the company's systems. Courses are available in Spanish; product names stay in English. Each course issues a certificate of completion. Courses and durations as at 26 August 2026.

Block 1 — Foundations · 7.5 hr

How a language model works, and how to work with one.

Course Lessons Time
AI Capabilities and Limitations13 + quiz3.5 hr
AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations14 + quiz4 hr

Next-token prediction, working memory, context limits, steerability. Then the 4D framework: delegation, description, discernment, diligence.

Block 2 — Claude app · 5 hr

Course Lessons Time
Claude 10113 + quiz2.5 hr
Introduction to Claude Cowork14 + quiz2.5 hr

Projects, artifacts, skills, connectors, enterprise search. Cowork covers handing Claude a whole task on real files, plus Claude for Microsoft 365 and Claude in Chrome.

Alongside these, the Academy carries use case modules of ten minutes each, tagged by function.
