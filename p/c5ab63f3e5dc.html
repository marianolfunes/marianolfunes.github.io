One project per tender

The pliego, the circulars, the offer and the notes for one tender in one shared Claude Project, worked by the team. It replaces the one shared NotebookLM licence the Comercial team described on 2-Sep-2026, and processes 3, 4, 7 and 8 work out of it. The go/no-go at 5 is the directorio's and the costeo at 6 stays in CyPres. Discussed with Nicolás Rossetti on 7-Sep-2026 and agreed as a starting point.

What has to be designed is the way of working — how the team works one tender when chats are private. Nicolás Rossetti, 7-Sep-2026: the progress has to live in the shared files inside the Project, not in anyone's conversation.

It also answers both questions the team asked on 2-Sep-2026. One shared document per project is the Project. On tracking of comments and modifications: a comment is a question, a risk or a decision on a clause, and it becomes a dated row in the requirements matrix, the consultas list or the circular register. It can be searched, and it stays when the document it came from is superseded.

Two classes of file.

Fixed — arrives once and is not edited afterwards: the pliego as issued, the technical specifications, the drawings, the annexes, and each circular as it arrives.

Live — rewritten as the tender moves: the requirements matrix, the ambiguities list, the consultas list, the circular register, the delivery checklist, the bitácora.

Tracking goes inside the live files. Anthropic's Projects documentation, read 7-Sep-2026, describes no version history for project knowledge. So each live file is one table, every row dated and initialled, and a row is superseded rather than deleted.

One owner per live file. Inference: if several people with edit rights re-upload the same matrix they overwrite each other, and the convention prevents it rather than the product.

Filenames carry meaning — circular-03.pdf, not escaneado.pdf. Anthropic, Retrieval augmented generation (RAG) for projects, read 7-Sep-2026: "Well-named files help Claude understand and retrieve the right information more effectively."

Chats are private. Anthropic, Manage project visibility and sharing, read 7-Sep-2026: chats inside a shared project are not visible to other members unless the author shares them. The group works from the files.

The structure comes from how a tender folder is laid out on their disc today. As a starting point:

  • Project instructions — what the tender is, who is on it, the standing rules
  • pliego-* · planos-* — fixed
  • circular-NN — fixed, one file each as they arrive
  • matriz-requisitos · ambiguedades · consultas · registro-circulares · checklist-entrega · bitacora — live
  • The offer as it firms up — planilla de cotización, memoria técnica, antecedentes — live until the Oferta, fixed after it

A template project. Same instructions, same empty live files, copied for each new tender.
