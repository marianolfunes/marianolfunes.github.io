Supercemento · August 2026

Executive summary

This document assesses the LLM applications suitable for individual productivity, as part of the AI integration plan for Supercemento.

Recommended: Claude Team. It ranks first on both axes assessed: power — the app, the harness and the models combined — and adoption by a non-technical user. It reaches Microsoft 365 through connectors: reads mail, calendar, files and Teams, writes to mail, calendar and files.

Assessment

Power: what can be achieved with the app, the harness and the models together.

Adoption: ease of adoption for a non-technical user — building workflows, organising work, templates, training.

Integration: native, or through connectors authorised in the tenant.
